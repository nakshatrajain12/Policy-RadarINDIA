### Methodology
Each scheme in the sample underwent programmatic validation across 5 criteria:
1. **Entity Verification**: Valid official central ministry, department, or state startup mission.
2. **Funding Accuracy**: Verified monetary cap or structured milestone tranche from official notifications.
3. **Stage Taxonomy**: Normalization into Idea, Prototype/MVP, Early Revenue, or Growth/Scale-up.
4. **Source Grounding**: Valid `.gov.in`, `.nic.in`, or recognized state nodal agency portal URL.
5. **Evidentiary Support**: Concise, factual summary extracted directly from public gazette/scheme guidelines without LLM hallucination.
